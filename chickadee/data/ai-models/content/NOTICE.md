Photo content classifier

ResNet18, three independent outputs: adult imagery, gambling, drugs. CPU inference; unknown labels masked during training.

Training sources: Figshare adult image dataset (research/non-commercial terms), SIMAS https://zenodo.org/records/15423637 (publisher non-commercial terms), Vanessa Garza Drug Detection legacy v3 https://universe.roboflow.com/vanessa-garza/drug-detection (CC BY 4.0). No raw images are redistributed. Trained model supplied for research and non-commercial use; source code Apache-2.0 does not replace dataset/model conditions.

Image category signals require review with the original page. Test and CPU validation details are recorded in manifest.json.
